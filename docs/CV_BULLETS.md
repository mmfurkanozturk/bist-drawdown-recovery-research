## Quant-focused version

**BIST 100 Drawdown & Recovery Research**  
- Designed a mechanical peak-to-trough drawdown detector on 7,794 BIST 100 daily observations, avoiding ex-post crisis selection in the primary sample.
- Backtested -10%/-15%/-20% limit entries and a 25%/35%/40% staged allocation across 36 completed ≥10% drawdowns.
- Extended the study with Turkey CPI/HICP purchasing-power breakeven, transaction-cost sensitivity, bootstrap/Wilson uncertainty analysis, and severity-conditioned recovery statistics.
- Found a strong regime split: staged median recovery was 6–24 days in 10–30% drawdowns versus 235 days in 30%+ drawdowns, highlighting structural-crisis tail risk.

## One-line project description

Quantitative event/drawdown study of BIST 100 recovery dynamics using observable entry rules, staged allocation, inflation-adjusted breakeven, and robustness analysis.
